## Decision rule

Each D0 item below records the frozen decision, its rationale, and acceptance tests. Fixed user requirements elsewhere in the specification are not relaxed by this record. Fold, purge/embargo, warm-up, sample, regime, cost, and acceptance thresholds are constants in the persistent search-family protocol and may not be adjusted after candidate outcomes are observed. Any approved pre-result correction requires a new protocol ID; prior attempts remain in the cumulative family, budget, ordinal sequence, and multiplicity denominator.

## D0 decisions

### D0-1 — Candidate grammar, search budget, and allocation

**Decision:** Permit only three finite declarative families: `regime_specialization`, `signal_combination`, and `regime_selection_allocation`. Across the persistent search family, cap attempts at 12 per family and 36 total. These are cumulative ceilings across every linked protocol/revision; failed or invalid attempts consume the budget, and no campaign restart replenishes it. Every generated or submitted candidate in an allocated slot becomes a manifest entry, including malformed, invalid, skipped, and never-evaluated candidates; an unfilled slot is not an attempt. Candidate grammar may select/abstain, combine existing frozen signals with declared Boolean rules, specialize by the frozen decision-time regime, or set deterministic priority among existing strategies. It may not change strategy code, thresholds, parameters, position sizing, leverage, risk limits, execution, or introduce adaptive/learned behavior. Weighted capital allocation is excluded; allocation-family candidates may only choose a strategy or deterministic priority under the frozen risk budget.

**Reason:** Bounded search makes trial accounting auditable; a finite grammar limits degrees of freedom. Capital weights confound signal-selection evidence with risk-budget changes and are deferred until candidate-level evidence exists.

**Acceptance tests:** Reject unknown grammar operators, numeric strategy/risk parameters, dynamic code, and weights; accept only the enumerated policies; enforce 12/36 limits cumulatively across linked protocols; require exactly one manifest row for every generated/submitted candidate slot.

### D0-2 — Nested chronological fold geometry

**Decision:** Use UTC half-open intervals and nested expanding rolling origin. Require at least 24 complete calendar months of training history; use 6 complete calendar months for each inner validation block; use 6 complete calendar months for each outer test block; advance outer origins by 6 complete calendar months. Apply a 96-expected-bar embargo from training outcomes to validation and from selection outcomes to outer test. Require at least six eligible, nonoverlapping outer test blocks for a historical walk-forward executable edge claim. This fold-count gate does not apply to the separate one-time prospective holdout, which has its own fixed-duration and sample gates below. Within each outer origin, candidate selection may use only inner folds ending before the outer test begins. Do not use outer results to revise the candidate and retain the same outer result as confirmatory evidence.

**Reason:** Twenty-four months includes two seasonal cycles before any historical selection. Six-month outer blocks preserve more chronological checkpoints than annual blocks while avoiding overlap in scored returns. The current span is long enough to investigate several such blocks, but all data through 2026-08-19 were previously observed, so the blocks remain historical diagnostics and are not genuine untouched OOS evidence. Expanding training periods overlap; only the scored outer blocks are disjoint.

**Acceptance tests:** Check UTC calendar boundary construction, strict ordering, minimum history, nonoverlapping outer test weeks, inner-fold containment before outer test, six-fold eligibility, and historical classification for all dates at or before 2026-08-19.

### D0-3 — Label purge, position horizon, embargo, and warm-up

**Decision:** Set maximum forward-label horizon `H_label = 96` contiguous expected 15-minute bars (24 hours). A label is eligible before boundary `B` only when its label endpoint is strictly earlier than `B`; otherwise purge it. For each scored six-calendar-month validation or outer-test interval `[S,E)`, prohibit new entries in the final 96 expected bars before `E`; existing positions continue under frozen Phase 4 exit rules and are force-closed at `E` under its existing end-of-run policy, with costs reported. The theoretical maximum held position is `H_position = N_interval` bars, where `N_interval` is the count of expected 15-minute intervals in `[S,E)`; the largest six-calendar-month interval (184 days) gives a cap of 17,664 bars (184 days). The cap follows from fold duration and end-of-fold accounting; it is not a new strategy time stop. Set embargo to 96 expected bars (24 elapsed hours) after the last training/selection outcome before the next scored interval. Gaps do not shorten elapsed time. Purge any label, position, funding accrual, or scored outcome crossing its applicable boundary. Use exactly 816 prior contiguous 15-minute bars for causal feature/regime warm-up, corresponding to 51 higher-timeframe 4-hour candles; reset the warm-up after a data gap and do not score warm-up rows.

**Reason:** The frozen signal labels end at 96 bars. The repo regime classifier requires 51 4-hour candles, making 816 contiguous 15-minute bars the reproducible maximum warm-up dependency. The Phase 4 simulator already forces positions closed at the end of a run; applying that unchanged at each isolated score-window boundary bounds position outcomes without inventing a candidate stop. The 96-bar embargo matches the maximum label horizon. Fold-start warm-up uses prior observable data only and contributes no outcome.

**Acceptance tests:** Inject labels/trades/funding that touch or cross boundary; prove strict purge; assert a 96-bar embargo at both train-to-validation and validation-to-test transitions using elapsed UTC time across gaps; assert no entry in the terminal 96 expected bars; confirm positions may survive that blackout, force closure and costs at fold end, and `H_position=N_interval` up to 17,664 bars; verify 816-bar prior-only warm-up and reset after gaps.

### D0-4 — Minimum sample and regime coverage

**Decision:** Eligibility floors are: predictive lane, at least 200 eligible decision opportunities and 30 occupied UTC weeks; independent counterfactual lane, at least 100 completed trades and 30 occupied entry weeks; executable historical walk-forward lane, at least 100 completed positions, 30 occupied trade weeks, and six eligible outer test folds; any regime-specific edge claim, at least 30 observations and 12 occupied weeks in that regime in addition to the applicable whole-lane floor. No separate numerical confidence-interval-width cutoff is used: uncertainty intervals and their width are always reported; if minimum samples pass but the adjusted lower bound does not clear zero, the claim is `REJECTED` as unproven; if a required estimate cannot be computed because sample/cluster coverage is insufficient, the outcome is `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`. Failure of a sample/coverage floor is never treated as `REJECTED` or as evidence of no edge.

**Reason:** Under an illustrative independent Bernoulli approximation, 200 observations give a 95% proportion half-width of about 6.9 percentage points near 50%; 100 give about 9.8 points. Financial observations are dependent, so these are screening floors only; weekly block-bootstrap intervals and occupied-week reporting remain mandatory. The executable historical Phase 4 ledger has 91 positions and does not meet the 100-position floor even before the 30-week and six-fold conditions. A regime with 30 observations still has substantial uncertainty; the 12-week requirement prevents one short cluster from being called regime coverage. None of these counts establishes power or independence.

**Acceptance tests:** Boundary values pass only at/above every applicable floor; below-floor completed runs are `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`; missing weeks do not count; regime claims require both regime and lane floors; reports display counts and warnings beside every estimate; no implementation may add or change a confidence-width cutoff after results are observed.

### D0-5 — Edge estimands and mandatory candidate/incumbent pairing

**Decision:** Pre-register four one-sided confirmatory hypotheses per candidate, with all measures defined on the same eligible UTC calendar weeks/folds:

1. Predictive absolute: the common opportunity universe is the set of unique UTC timestamps at which any of the four frozen raw strategies emits a signal. Every candidate and the incumbent produces at most one direction or abstains at each same timestamp. For opportunity `i`, the direction-adjusted 96-bar label score is `s_i = d_i * (P[i+96]/P_ref[i] - 1)`, where `d_i ∈ {-1,+1}` is the selected signal direction and `P_ref` is the frozen Phase 5 signal-close reference price. Abstention scores zero. Weekly score is the arithmetic mean over all eligible opportunity timestamps in that UTC week; empty weeks are zero. `theta_pred_abs` is the arithmetic mean of weekly scores.
2. Predictive incremental: at every same opportunity timestamp, compare candidate score with the incumbent's score, assigning zero to abstention. `theta_pred_delta` is the arithmetic mean of paired weekly candidate-minus-incumbent scores.
3. Executable absolute: `R[c,w] = equity_end[c,w] / equity_start[c,w] - 1` from the candidate's fresh independent ledger. Include zero-trade weeks at return zero. `theta_exec_abs` is the mean weekly portfolio return under each predeclared cost scenario; robust absolute estimand is the minimum of baseline and all three one-factor adverse-cost means.
4. Executable incremental: `theta_exec_delta` is the arithmetic mean of paired weekly `R[candidate,w] - R[incumbent,w]` under the exact baseline Phase 4 cost and execution policy.

All candidate/incumbent runs must use identical input hashes, opportunity universe, folds, warm-up, scored calendar weeks, Phase 4 configuration, initial equity, costs, fills, funding, risk caps, and fold-end policy. Each candidate and each incumbent control is replayed into a distinct fresh portfolio ledger. The frozen Phase 4 ledger is an immutable reference and never an evaluation ledger. Executable advancement requires both adjusted absolute executable evidence above zero and adjusted paired executable improvement above zero. A less-negative candidate cannot qualify. Add a fold concentration guard: for weekly candidate returns `R_w`, let `P_f=Σ_{w∈f}max(R_w,0)` for outer fold `f` and `P=Σ_w max(R_w,0)`; require `max_f(P_f/P)≤0.50` when `P>0`. The threshold prevents one of at least six outer folds from supplying a majority of all positive weekly return contribution. A predictive edge claim separately requires both predictive hypotheses to pass; predictive evidence cannot substitute for executable evidence, and counterfactual evidence is descriptive and cannot qualify either claim. Report the lane outcomes independently; do not collapse them into a combined score.

**Reason:** Weekly portfolio return is the unit aligned with dependence and paired allocation decisions. Zero scores for abstentions prevent selecting only favorable labels. Requiring absolute and incremental evidence prevents ranking among losing alternatives from being described as an edge.

**Acceptance tests:** Recompute estimands and fold concentration from fixtures; reject concentration above 0.50; assert paired candidate/control week keys; reject any mismatched assumption metadata or reused ledger path; show candidate loses less but remains nonqualifying when absolute lower bound is not positive.

### D0-6 — Costs, uncertainty, and multiplicity

**Decision:** Baseline executable costs are byte-identical to frozen Phase 4 configuration (`bf5eeff4de2b58487f917cefecd9b8b7dc94ec7a72bf125729f34ab65c861627`): 5 bps per-side fee, 5 bps per-side adverse slippage, 1 bp funding per 8 hours, maximum 5% participation, and all other frozen execution/risk settings. Run three separate one-factor stress cases: fee 10 bps/side with all else baseline; slippage 10 bps/side with all else baseline; funding 2 bps/8h with all else baseline. Never combine stress factors silently and never use zero-cost performance for qualification.

Use paired, non-circular moving-block bootstrap over UTC calendar weeks, fixed block length 4 weeks, 100,000 replicates, two-sided 95% percentile intervals, and one-sided lower bounds for gates. Resample candidate and incumbent week vectors together; within each replicate use the same sampled week blocks for all scenarios and candidates. For estimate `theta_hat`, let `theta_b*` be bootstrap replicate `b`, center the null-error replicate `e_b=theta_b*−theta_hat`, and calculate the positive-edge one-sided p-value as `(1 + count(e_b >= theta_hat))/(100000 + 1)`. For the robust absolute estimand, compute `theta_b*` as the minimum across baseline and all three scenario means using the same sampled blocks. Holm-adjust these raw p-values across the search family; derive simultaneous lower bounds by inversion of the Holm-adjusted tests. The 100,000-replicate p-value resolution (1/100001) is materially finer than the strictest first-step Holm cutoff at 144 hypotheses (0.05/144 ≈ 0.000347); report Monte Carlo tail-count uncertainty. Deterministic seeds are protocol/attempt-bound. Define one persistent `search_family_id` as the SHA-256 JCS content ID of the root hypothesis, three allowed families, and frozen benchmark/config hashes; preserve it for every linked protocol/revision. Apply Holm step-down family-wise error control at alpha 0.05 across that complete persistent family: every attempt in every linked protocol/revision and all four confirmatory hypotheses per attempt. No protocol revision, restart, new campaign, or failed run resets this family; a separate family cannot be declared to shed prior attempts within the same Athena strategy-search objective. The global budget caps the family at 36 attempts, hence at most 144 hypotheses. Every invalid, failed, skipped, unevaluated, insufficient-evidence, or missing-statistic attempt contributes p=1 and remains in the correction denominator. The incumbent is a comparator, not another candidate hypothesis. Independent counterfactual metrics and unregistered subgroup analyses are descriptive/exploratory and cannot qualify.

**Reason:** Phase 4 supplies the executable base assumptions; one-factor stresses identify cost fragility without introducing an arbitrary bundled shock. Search-family-wide Holm control prevents family/protocol shopping and preserves strong family-wise error control without assuming independent tests. Four-week blocks reflect Phase 5's existing weekly-cluster method and retain short-run clustering. The 100,000 resamples provide p-value resolution below the most stringent Holm threshold for 144 hypotheses; this differs from Phase 5's 2,000 descriptive resamples because Phase 5 did not need this multiple-testing resolution. Bootstrap tails and percentile intervals remain approximations, so sample warnings remain required.

**Acceptance tests:** Exact baseline hash and stress scenario assertions; identical pairing under bootstrap; deterministic intervals; 100,000-replicate p-value resolution; known centered-bootstrap and Holm fixtures; invalid/failed/unevaluated p=1 placeholders; four hypotheses per attempt; adding a linked protocol/revision increases the same search-family denominator and cannot reset prior p-values; no zero-cost qualification.

### D0-7 — Candidate/protocol identity and deterministic seeds

**Decision:** Candidate and protocol IDs are `sha256:` plus SHA-256 of RFC 8785 JCS semantic payload. Exclude identity field itself, creation timestamp, and display-only name; include all decision-affecting fields, parent IDs, baseline hashes, and schema version. The protocol ID excludes only derived `candidate_set.attempt_manifest_sha256`, because that digest contains attempt IDs that themselves contain the protocol ID; the full manifest digest is instead bound by the hash-chained `CANDIDATE_SET_SEALED` event in the same atomic transaction. Revisions that change semantics create new immutable IDs and link parent lineage. Each attempt ID hashes canonical JSON containing protocol ID, one-based ordinal globally monotonic within the persistent search family, assigned family, and SHA-256 of exact submitted candidate-spec bytes. The ordinal never resets across protocols. Master seed is `20261118`; derive each statistical seed from SHA-256 of the UTF-8 string `protocol_id|attempt_id|fold_id|metric_id|20261118`; interpret the first eight digest bytes as unsigned big-endian integer. No strategy-decision randomness is permitted.

**Reason:** Content IDs make semantic identity independent of pretty-printing while preserving raw submitted bytes for attempt audit. Attempt-scoped seeds prevent candidate ID reuse from conflating retries. Fixed seed provenance supports cross-platform replay.

**Acceptance tests:** Published golden vectors for candidate, protocol, attempt, and seed IDs; semantic change changes ID; display-only change does not; identical hashes reproduce all resamples; retry has a distinct attempt ordinal/ID.

### D0-8 — Registry lifecycle and accounting of attempts

**Decision:** Seal a manifest before parsing candidate specs, checking lineage, evaluating, or exposing validation/test outcomes. One manifest row is mandatory for every generated or submitted candidate attempt; an empty, unfilled generator slot is not an attempt. Registry history is append-only and includes invalid, failed, rejected, insufficient, skipped, and unevaluated candidates. Status mapping is fixed: schema/grammar/lineage failure → `INVALID_CANDIDATE`; completed evaluation below sample/coverage floor or with uncomputable interval due to too few clusters → `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`; infrastructure/resource/hash/accounting/invariant failure → `TECHNICAL_EVALUATION_FAILURE`; valid completed evidence that fails a scientific gate (including adjusted bounds not above zero or fold concentration >0.50) → `REJECTED`; candidate sealed but never evaluated → `UNEVALUATED`. These statuses are never collapsed. Re-run is a new attempt. Missing or noncomputable p-value is conservatively 1; post-result protocol amendment cannot erase or reset prior family attempts.

**Acceptance tests:** Manifest completeness and immutability, status-reason mapping, append-only event hash chain, retry lineage, and denominator invariance under aborts or invalid submissions.

### D0-9 — Compute, storage, and fail-closed limits

**Decision:** Run offline and serially (one worker). Hard cumulative caps across the persistent search family: 1,800 seconds wall-clock per candidate attempt; 86,400 seconds aggregate evaluator wall time; 8,192 MiB peak resident memory per process; 536,870,912 bytes (512 MiB) per attempt's derived artifacts; 10,737,418,240 bytes (10 GiB) aggregate derived artifacts. Protocol revisions do not reset aggregate time or artifact use. Exceeding a limit stops the attempt, records `TECHNICAL_EVALUATION_FAILURE`, retains the attempt in multiplicity, and prohibits partial metrics from being labeled complete. Write only into a newly created dated run directory; never overwrite. Refuse any output destination within frozen source/data/report roots.

**Reason:** Explicit caps make resource failures deterministic and prevent unbounded output. Serial execution reduces nondeterminism and oversubscription. Caps are deliberately finite and should be measured against the pinned clean environment before implementation; changing them requires pre-registration, not an in-run extension.

**Acceptance tests:** Wall, memory, per-attempt and aggregate search-family artifact/time boundary fixtures; linked protocol revisions share the same counters; offline/network denial; exclusive directory creation; path traversal and symlink escape rejection; incomplete artifacts cannot publish complete result status.

### D0-10 — Prospective holdout isolation and one-time use

**Decision:** All existing data through 2026-08-19 are previously observed and permanently ineligible to be called an untouched holdout. Before prospective accrual starts, lock the exact UTC holdout start and end, with at least 52 complete weeks plus the trailing 96-bar label horizon; do not extend the window in response to sample count or interim outcomes. Require at least 200 predictive opportunities across 30 occupied weeks for a predictive claim, at least 100 executable positions across 30 occupied weeks for an executable claim, and 30 observations across 12 occupied weeks for any regime-specific claim. If a floor is missed, report `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`; no extension is made under that protocol. The six-outer-fold requirement applies to historical walk-forward evidence, not this single one-time prospective block; no interim holdout results may be used for revision. The generator, candidate selector, and ordinary evaluator have no access. Only a separate read-only confirmatory evaluator can access it after the protocol ID/hash, source commit, full sealed attempt-manifest hash, candidate hashes, exact holdout boundaries, fold rules, preprocessing, costs, estimands, seed rules, and acceptance gates are locked. A named independent custodian releases access once; any inspection consumes the holdout. It cannot return intermediate results to generation or selection. If any candidate/protocol changes after access, the consumed data become previously observed and a new future holdout requires separate approval. No custodian is appointed in this specification; appointment is a precondition to any holdout staging.

**Reason:** This prevents historical information from being relabeled and protects against adaptive reuse of supposedly confirmatory outcomes. A pre-locked 52-week minimum supports a full seasonal cycle; if it misses sample floors, the answer is insufficient evidence rather than outcome-dependent extension. Requiring six future rolling folds would consume several years and reuse a single sealed period, so the prospective holdout is evaluated once as a fixed block and must meet the explicit position/opportunity floors instead.

**Acceptance tests:** Deny mount/read at generation, selection, and ordinary-evaluation boundaries; enforce pre-locked exact boundaries, the 52-week plus 96-bar-tail, and lane/regime opportunity floors without requiring six holdout folds; reject sample-driven extension; permit exactly one hash-verified read-only opening; consume and audit access even on a failed attempt; deny changed hashes and second opening.

### D0-11 — Qualification, evidence conclusion, and human authority

**Decision:** Candidate status after passing historical executable gates is only `RESEARCH_ADVANCED_PENDING_PROSPECTIVE`; any predictive-edge claim is labeled separately and only when both predictive hypotheses pass. Prospective confirmation status remains pending until the isolated holdout independently passes the same registered lane-specific absolute, paired, cost-robust (where applicable), sample, uncertainty, and multiplicity gates. A human may append `APPROVED_FOR_FURTHER_RESEARCH` only. No status can modify source, frozen config, portfolio ledger, paper/live runner, or production allocation. Emit `NO_QUALIFYING_CANDIDATE` when no registered candidate meets the executable advancement gates; separately report if no candidate demonstrates predictive edge. The engine may conclude, with scope explicitly limited to available previously observed evidence: “No existing strategy or registered candidate has demonstrated a robust predictive and executable edge under this protocol.” It may not infer that no strategy can ever work.

**Reason:** Human research approval is not deployment authority. The valid null conclusion prevents forced selection from a pool of losing candidates and is consistent with Phase 5's negative counterfactual net expectancy and insufficient historical executable sample.

**Acceptance tests:** No candidate passes on rank alone; all promotion-like statuses are rejected; human approval appends only a research event; zero qualifiers yields `NO_QUALIFYING_CANDIDATE`; evidence-bound wording cannot be broadened into a universal claim.
