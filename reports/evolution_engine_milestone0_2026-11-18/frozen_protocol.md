Changing any value after candidate results are observed invalidates the affected protocol; prior attempts remain in the same search family and multiplicity denominator.

## Outcome and evidence rules

`NO_QUALIFYING_CANDIDATE` means the complete, valid registered search finished and no candidate passed every preregistered gate. It is a successful terminal research result, not an evaluator error, not `TECHNICAL_EVALUATION_FAILURE`, and not a reason to relax thresholds. The registry payload records `run_status=COMPLETED_VALID`, `qualifying_candidate_count=0`, total registered attempts, `all_attempts_resolved_terminally=true`, `technical_failure_count=0`, `unevaluated_count=0`, `acceptance_criteria_unchanged=true`, and the fixed reason `no_candidate_met_all_preregistered_qualification_gates`. Registry-level validation reconciles these fields against all sealed manifests and attempt events. The terminal payload schema rejects additional fields, including error or threshold-relaxation fields.

The three lanes remain independent: `predictive_signal`, `independent_counterfactual`, and `executable_portfolio`. A predictive pass does not establish executable performance; counterfactual results are descriptive; executable qualification requires both absolute after-cost profitability and paired improvement. Candidate and incumbent use identical input hashes, folds, eligible times, warm-up, costs, Phase 4 config, execution model, initial equity, funding, risk controls, and fold-end rules, with independent fresh ledgers.

Candidate result statuses are distinct: `INVALID_CANDIDATE`, `UNEVALUATED`, `TECHNICAL_EVALUATION_FAILURE`, `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`, and `REJECTED`. Missing p-values receive p=1. Repeated protocols, commits, or runs retain the same `search_family_id`, attempt budget, global ordinal sequence, and multiplicity denominator.

## Numerical lock and change control

All numeric constants and formulas in this document are frozen for the M0 design. They are represented as constants in `experiment_protocol.schema.json`; cross-run limits, cumulative counts, fold chronology, event lifecycle, and access control are enforced by the acceptance tests. Before candidate results exist, any requested change requires a new protocol revision and review; after any candidate result is observed, a change cannot erase prior attempts or reset budgets or multiplicity. No future evaluator may infer or tune thresholds from the result data.
